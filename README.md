## Presentation Details
- Duration: 15 minutes for presentation + 5 minutes for Q&A
- Format: Group presentation (all members must present)

### The presentation should cover the following aspects
- The problem statement
- Theoretical working of an already available system
- Your plans and objectives to address the problem
- Source of data
- Infrastructure and models
- Decision-making responsibilities (who will make decisions)
- Potential beneficiaries of the DSS

After the presentation, both I and the rest of the class will ask questions related to your project. Finally, I will provide my feedback on whether the proposed project is sufficient or if further improvements are needed.
